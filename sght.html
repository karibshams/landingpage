About Shafer Gaston

Shafer Gaston is a dedicated public servant committed to bringing positive change to our community. With years of experience in local government and community organizing, Shafer understands the challenges facing our district.

Born and raised in our community, Shafer has witnessed firsthand the need for effective leadership that puts people first. Their background in public policy and grassroots advocacy has prepared them to tackle the issues that matter most to working families.

When elected, Shafer will focus on creating jobs, improving infrastructure, and ensuring that every voice is heard in the halls of government.

Key Issues

Economic Development

Creating good-paying jobs and supporting local businesses through targeted investment and reduced bureaucratic barriers. We need to foster an environment where entrepreneurs can thrive while ensuring workers have access to fair wages and benefits.

Infrastructure

Modernizing our roads, bridges, and public transportation to support economic growth and improve quality of life. Our aging infrastructure needs immediate attention to ensure safety and efficiency for all residents.

Education

Ensuring our schools have the resources they need to prepare students for the jobs of tomorrow. Every child deserves access to quality education regardless of their zip code or family income.

Healthcare Access

Working to expand affordable healthcare options for all residents, regardless of income level. Healthcare is a right, not a privilege, and we must work to make it accessible to everyone in our community.
